Direct API authorization evidence (the server enforces roles and ownership, not the UI)
Status codes follow the ladder 401 -> 403 -> 400 -> 404 -> 409.

1. No session opens a staff ticket
    GET /api/staff/tickets/4753
    -> 401  {"error":"Authentication required."}

2. A Requester opens a staff ticket
    GET /api/staff/tickets/4753
    -> 403  {"error":"You do not have permission to perform this action."}

3. A Requester claims a ticket
    POST /api/staff/tickets/4753/claim
    -> 403  {"error":"You do not have permission to perform this action."}

4. A Requester reads Internal Notes of their own ticket (no note content returned)
    GET /api/tickets/4753/notes
    -> 403  {"error":"You do not have permission to perform this action."}

5. A Requester writes an Internal Note
    POST /api/tickets/4753/notes  {"body":"x"}
    -> 403  {"error":"You do not have permission to perform this action."}

6. An Administrator may READ a staff ticket (read-only)
    GET /api/staff/tickets/4753
    -> 200  {"ticket":{"id":4753,"ticketNumber":"TKT-E2E-000001","requesterId":307,"categoryId":1,"relatedSystemId":1,"summary":"[e2e] Staff flow ticket…

7. An Administrator claims a ticket
    POST /api/staff/tickets/4753/claim
    -> 403  {"error":"You do not have permission to perform this action."}

8. An Administrator changes a status
    PATCH /api/staff/tickets/4753/status  {"currentStatus":"CLOSED","confirm":true}
    -> 403  {"error":"You do not have permission to perform this action."}

9. An Administrator writes an Internal Note
    POST /api/tickets/4753/notes  {"body":"x"}
    -> 403  {"error":"You do not have permission to perform this action."}

10. IT Staff change status without the required confirmation (validation, 400)
    PATCH /api/staff/tickets/4753/status  {"currentStatus":"CLOSED"}
    -> 400  {"errors":{"confirm":"Confirmation is required to set this status."}}

11. IT Staff make a status change the workflow does not allow (conflict, 409)
    PATCH /api/staff/tickets/4753/status  {"currentStatus":"OPEN"}
    -> 409  {"error":"Cannot transition from RESOLVED to OPEN."}

12. IT Staff claim a ticket that already has an owner (conflict, 409)
    POST /api/staff/tickets/4753/claim
    -> 409  {"error":"Ticket is already assigned. Use assign to change its owner."}

13. IT Staff ask for a ticket that does not exist (404)
    GET /api/staff/tickets/999999
    -> 404  {"error":"Ticket not found."}

14. A forged Origin on a state-changing call is refused (403)
    PATCH /api/staff/tickets/4753/priority  {"itPriority":"LOW"}
    -> 403  {"error":"Request origin not allowed."}

15. A Requester opens ANOTHER Requester's ticket: 404, not 403 (no existence leak)
    GET /api/tickets/3502
    -> 404  {"error":"Ticket not found."}
